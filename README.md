An entry for make.fun's "The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head"
(u-kevin-the-funniest-office-joke-judged-head-to-head-3dc3233c).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes one fixed one-line joke
about office life to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no randomness, no input:
the same bytes on every run.

## The joke

The office plant died, so facilities replaced it with a laminated photo of a
plant and a ticket to water it.

The line was picked by a local pairwise tournament over 20 original candidates,
judged against the bounty brief: a real joke with a setup and a turn, built on
something anyone who has worked in an office recognises, with a surprise turn
and the punchline last.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
